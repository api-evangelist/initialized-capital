Why Initialized led Garage's seed round and participated in its Series A for $18M total funding. America’s local governments and public safety departments are the backbone of our communities, yet the way they buy and sell the most essential equipment needed for doing their jobs hasn’t changed in decades. Garage is a modern, AI-powered marketplace designed to streamline how specialized equipment is bought and sold.
